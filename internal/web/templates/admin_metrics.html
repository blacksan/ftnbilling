How these are measured

Download is traffic to the house, upload is from it. Per-house bytes come from the router's per-house queue counters once a minute; a restart of this app can lose up to a minute. Time at cap is the share of the house's online time in which its download averaged 90% or more of its package speed. Houses above {{f0 (index .Data.C "CapShare")}}% (after at least {{index .Data.C "MinOnline"}} minutes online) are upgrade candidates.

Services come from the router's traffic-flow export and are guessed from DNS names, then from ports. They are a good guide, not an exact count: devices that use their own encrypted DNS (DNS-over-HTTPS) show up as "HTTPS (other)", and the router must not fast-track customer traffic or it is invisible to traffic-flow.

Fair-use limit counts WAN download plus upload for the calendar month (EAT) since this app began collecting; it turns amber above {{f0 (index .Data.C "FUPWarn")}}%. The projection assumes the average pace so far continues.

Flag thresholds: WAN above {{f0 (index .Data.C "WANUtil")}}% for {{index .Data.C "WANHours"}} or more hours in 7 days; houses above {{f0 (index .Data.C "CapShare")}}% of time at cap; loss above {{f0 (index .Data.C "Loss")}}%.
